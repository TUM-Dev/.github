# Open Source @ TUM e.V.
**university group at the Technical University of Munich**

Welcome to our GitHub org!
Here we build and collaborating on things to make our universitys infrastrucutre better as developers, designers, researchers, and makers.

## Who We Are

We are a university group at the Technical University of Munich that works on various open source projects.
We develop software, organise events and offer workshops.
Our projects are freely available to everyone and we welcome any support.
For more information about us and about the projects we have, please see https://www.tum.dev/

> [!TIP]
> If you would like to join us, why don't you come along to one of our meetings?
> Just contact the leads at leads {at-symbol} tum.dev, we look forward to hearing from you!
